Designing customizable card decks.

Brand identity, custom type, card styles and product design for MyCardDeck.

RoleProduct designer · Co-founder
Websitemycarddeck.com ↗
The concept

Custom decks from your own photos.

We wanted people to create a deck from their photos. I designed the brand, cards and product flow for it.

01
Upload photos

The user starts with their own images.

02
Choose the design

The user selects a style, layout, background and back cover.

03
Order the deck

The user reviews the deck and places the order.

01 / Identity

Logo and type.

Leilei
Custom display typePlayful / medieval / magic

I constructed each letter and number vectorially, inspired by other typefaces but shaped into a voice of its own: playful, slightly medieval and a little magical.

03 / Back covers

Back cover options.

I designed multiple back covers so users can choose the one that fits their deck.

02 / AI card styles

AI-generated card styles.

I tested male, female and animal references across six styles. Each prompt needed corrections to keep the suits, figures, numbers and layouts consistent.

04 / Product application

Product mockups.

I used mockups to show the result before users started editing. The goal was to make the options easier to understand for people without design experience.

05 / Editor

The editor.

The editor breaks customisation into steps: upload images, choose a card style and layout, then choose the background and back cover.
